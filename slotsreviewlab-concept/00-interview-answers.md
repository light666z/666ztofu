## Audience

1. The primary audience is Korean adults who are interested in slot games and are looking for interesting games worth checking out based on their visual design, features, themes, and overall experience.

2. Readers want an easy way to discover and evaluate interesting slot games without having to research each game themselves. The blog should make their visit worthwhile by highlighting each game's visual design, sounds, features, mechanics, bonus features, and other unique characteristics.

3. The primary target is Korean readers rather than foreign-language audiences because the blog will be written primarily in Korean. However, there are no strict restrictions on who may access or read the content.

4. The content should be accessible to beginners while still providing enough detail for readers with prior experience. It should not assume a specific level of knowledge, and unfamiliar game terminology should be explained when necessary.

## Purpose

5. SlotReviewLab should help Korean readers discover and explore featured slot games by providing clear and interesting reviews of their visual design, themes, features, mechanics, and overall experience.

6. Success after 90 days would primarily mean establishing a consistent publishing process, building a clear identity and content direction for SlotReviewLab, and having a substantial collection of quality articles covering the site's main topics. Reader feedback is welcome but is not a primary success metric.

7. The blog should never become a source of misleading gambling claims, guaranteed-win promises, or content that presents slot games as a reliable way to make money. It should remain focused on informative and interesting game reviews rather than aggressive gambling promotion.

## Voice

8. The tone should be calm, playful, and somewhat academic. Reviews should be informative and interesting without being overly serious. The content should avoid an overly strict or authoritative tone, especially because slot outcomes are inherently volatile and the reviews should not be presented as absolute judgments or guarantees.

9. The reader-facing content should be primarily natural Korean. English may be used when appropriate for technical terms, brand names, and commonly used terminology. For game titles, when an official or commonly used Korean name exists, the Korean name should be included alongside the original English title so readers can easily recognize both. The order of the Korean and English names can depend on which version is more commonly recognized.

10. The review should lean toward a recommendation-first approach, but recommendations should be based on the game's design, theme, visuals, sounds, features, mechanics, and overall experience rather than its ability to produce wins. The content should not imply or guarantee that readers will win money.

## Topics

11. The main focus should be slot game reviews and exploration. This includes visual design, themes, sounds, game mechanics, bonus features, bonus buys, and other unique characteristics of each game. Related content can be considered if it helps readers discover and understand slot games.

12. There are no strict topic exclusions at this stage, but the primary focus should remain on slot games and closely related content. Topics unrelated to slot games should not be prioritized simply for traffic or SEO.

13. A good review should highlight the game's visual design, theme, sound and feedback, core features, mechanics, bonus features, and any notable or unique elements. Bonus-buy features should be discussed when the game offers them. The review should focus on explaining what makes the game interesting rather than promising financial results.

14. For the initial version, legality and access should be treated carefully and at a high level rather than being a primary content focus. The blog should focus primarily on reviewing and exploring the games themselves. Specific information about where to play can be considered later if needed.

## Constraints

15. The site should never make guaranteed-win claims, misleading claims about the probability of winning, or imply that a particular slot can reliably generate profit. Display advertisements for our existing sites may appear on the site, but they should remain separate from the editorial content and should not influence the accuracy or opinions expressed in the reviews.

16. I don't currently have specific competitors or content styles that I want to emulate or avoid. Relevant examples can be researched and discussed later rather than assumed during the initial planning stage.

17. The blog will be monetized/promoted primarily through display advertising for our existing sites, such as banner images or GIFs placed in areas like the sidebar. The article content itself should remain independent and focused on providing useful and interesting slot-related information, without being written around or affiliated with the advertised sites.

## Ops reality

18. Content will be AI-assisted with human editing. I will provide the final approval and want the AI's output to be presented in a concise format that makes it easy for me to review, approve, or reject.

19. The target publishing pace is daily, but this is not a strict requirement. The priority is maintaining consistent publishing rather than forcing a specific number of posts. Increasing the pace should not come at the expense of review quality or human approval.

20. Drafts will primarily be based on our notes, available game information, and play experience where applicable. Official sources may be used to verify specific game features and factual information. Before publishing, the content must be factually accurate, clearly written, relevant to the game being reviewed, and free from misleading claims or guaranteed-win language.
