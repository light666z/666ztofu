# 00 — Discovery Interview Questions

Blog: **slotreviewlab**  
Domain candidate: **slotreviewlab.com**  
Purpose of this file: capture owner intent before any further strategy work.  
Rule: do not invent answers; wait for the owner’s response.

---

## Audience

1. Who is the *single* primary reader you most want to serve in the first 90 days — not “everyone interested in slots,” but one specific type of person (age range, situation, and what they’re trying to do)?
2. What problem are they trying to solve when they land on slotreviewlab — and what would make them feel the visit was worth it?
3. Who is explicitly *not* your reader (people or intents you do not want to attract)?
4. How much prior knowledge should we assume — total beginner, some experience, or already familiar with game jargon?

## Purpose

5. In one sentence, what should slotreviewlab *exist to do* for that reader?
6. After 90 days, what concrete outcome would make you say “this is working” — beyond traffic numbers (e.g. trust, clarity of niche, reader feedback, publishing consistency)?
7. What should this blog *never* become, even if that path looked easy for clicks or money?

## Voice

8. If the blog were a person explaining slots to a friend, how should it sound (pick a few words: calm, sharp, playful, strict, academic, street-smart, etc.) — and which of those must it avoid?
9. Since the primary audience is Korean, should the reader-facing content be primarily natural Korean, with English used when appropriate for game titles, brand names, technical terms, or commonly used terminology?
10. On reviews, should the tone feel more like a lab report (criteria-first), a consumer guide (recommendation-first), or something else you can name?

## Topics

11. Which topics are clearly *in* for v1 (the ones you’d be disappointed not to see covered)?
12. Which topics are clearly *out* for v1 — even if related to gaming, gambling, or “slots” loosely?
13. When you say “review,” what does a good review need to include for you (and what is optional fluff)?
14. How should the blog treat legality / access / “where to play” for a South Korea audience — discuss openly, keep high-level only, or avoid that angle unless asked later?

## Constraints

15. What must never appear on the site (claims, angles, imagery, monetization tactics, or subject matter)?
16. Are there any competitors, sites, or content styles you admire — and any you actively do *not* want to resemble?
17. Any hard limits on commercial intent for the first 90 days (no affiliate, affiliate OK with disclosure, ads later, sponsorships forbidden, etc.)?

## Ops reality

18. Who will actually write and edit the posts in the first 90 days (you, a hired writer, AI-assisted with human edit, a team) — and who gives final approval?
19. What publishing pace is realistic with that setup — per week or per month — and what would break if we aimed higher?
20. Where will drafts, facts, and images come from (your notes, official sources, play experience, translation, stock, etc.), and what quality bar is non-negotiable before publish?
