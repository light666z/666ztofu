## Niche statement

slotreviewlab covers **slot games as a product category**: game design, volatility, RTP concepts, providers, feature types, and practical review frameworks for Korean readers.

We are **not** a “best casino bonus” spam site. We are a **lab-style review publication** that teaches readers how to evaluate slots before they care about where (or whether) to play.

### Niche boundaries

**In scope**
- Slot game reviews and comparisons
- Mechanics explainers (페이라인, 프리스핀, 멀티플라이어, 보너스 구매 등)
- Provider / studio overviews
- How to read RTP, volatility, hit frequency (개념 설명)
- Responsible play and risk literacy for Korean readers
- Market context relevant to Korean consumers (legal awareness, common scams, terminology)

**Out of scope (v1)**
- Sports betting deep-dives
- Poker strategy curricula
- Crypto trading / investment content
- Pure clickbait ranking lists with no review criteria
- Content that instructs readers how to bypass Korean gambling laws

## Positioning

### Category
Korean-language slot review & education media

### Differentiator
Most KR slot content is either translated affiliate copy or opaque “추천” lists. slotreviewlab differentiates with:

1. **Transparent scoring** — every review uses the same criteria
2. **Explain-first** — readers learn *why* a game scores as it does
3. **Korea-first framing** — terminology, examples, and risk notes tuned for KR readers
4. **Lab tone** — measured, evidence-minded, low hype

### Positioning sentence
> For Korean readers who want to understand slot games clearly, slotreviewlab is the review lab that explains mechanics and scores titles with transparent criteria — unlike typical affiliate blogs that push bonuses first.

## Brand personality

| Trait | Meaning in practice |
|-------|---------------------|
| Analytical | Prefer criteria, tables, and trade-offs over adjectives |
| Calm | No shouting CTAs, no fake urgency |
| Practical | Every post should help a reader decide or understand something |
| Transparent | Disclose limits of data; say when something is opinion |
| Responsible | Never romanticize losses or “sure win” language |

### Voice do / don’t

**Do**
- Use plain Korean with precise technical terms in English where natural (RTP, volatility, provider names)
- Show scoring breakdowns
- Compare alternatives fairly

**Don’t**
- Promise profits or “필승”
- Use fear/FOMO copy (“지금 안 하면 손해”)
- Pretend every game is “최고”
- Hide commercial relationships

## Visual direction (for later build)

Not a full design system yet — only brand cues for future UI:

- Feel: clean lab / notebook, not neon casino
- Avoid: purple-glow gambling clichés, jackpot-explosion imagery as primary brand
- Primary signals: clear typography, structured score cards, high-contrast readability for long Korean text
- Accent idea: cool steel blue + charcoal (working note), with restrained highlight color for scores

## Brand test (90-day)

A new visitor landing on the homepage should answer all three within 10 seconds:

1. This site is about **slot game reviews/education**
2. It is written for **Korean readers**
3. It feels **trustworthy and criteria-based**, not spammy
