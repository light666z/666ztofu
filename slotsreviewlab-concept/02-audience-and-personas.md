# 02 — Audience & Personas

## Primary audience (one sentence)

Korean readers and consumers in South Korea who want clear, useful information about slot games relevant to the Korean market — without being pushed by noisy affiliate marketing.

---

## Audience principles

1. **Korea-first value** — topics must matter to KR readers’ real questions, not just global SEO volume
2. **Literacy over lure** — help people understand games before chasing offers
3. **Flexible scope, fixed usefulness** — niche can expand later, but every post must earn trust now
4. **Adult audience** — content assumes 19+ readers; no youth targeting

---

## Jobs to be done

| Job | Reader need | Content that helps |
|-----|-------------|--------------------|
| Understand | “이 용어/메커니즘이 뭐지?” | Explainers, glossaries |
| Evaluate | “이 게임이 나한테 맞나?” | Structured reviews |
| Compare | “A와 B 중 뭐가 나을까?” | Comparisons, roundups with criteria |
| Avoid harm | “사기/과몰입/과장 정보는?” | Responsible play + scam literacy |
| Orient | “한국에서 이 주제를 어떻게 봐야 하지?” | Market-context primers |

## Personas

### Persona A — 미나, the cautious beginner
- **Age**: late 20s–30s
- **Context**: Curious about slot culture and game mechanics after seeing social content
- **Goal**: Understand basics without being scammed or pressured
- **Frustrations**: Confusing English jargon, aggressive “추천” blogs, unclear legality
- **Content she needs first**: Beginner glossary, volatility explainer, “how we review” page, soft first reviews

### Persona B — 준호, the informed comparer
- **Age**: 30s–40s
- **Context**: Already knows popular titles; wants sharper evaluation criteria
- **Goal**: Compare providers/features and decide what style he prefers
- **Frustrations**: Reviews that only restate trailer features; no scoring consistency
- **Content he needs**: Deep reviews, provider overviews, feature-type comparisons

### Persona C — 서연, the risk-aware adult
- **Age**: 30s–50s
- **Context**: Interested in entertainment info but highly sensitive to addiction/scam risk
- **Goal**: Learn responsibly and recognize red flags
- **Frustrations**: Sites that glamorize winning and hide loss potential
- **Content she needs**: Responsible play guides, scam patterns, “what RTP actually means”

---

## Audience insights for Korean market

- Many readers encounter slot content via **translated affiliate pages** and distrust them
- English terms (RTP, Megaways, Buy Bonus) are common; good KR content should **explain, not erase** them
- Legal and social sensitivity is high — trust grows when a site acknowledges risk and uncertainty honestly
- Readers value **clear structure** (목차, 표, 점수) for scanning on mobile

---

## Success signals from the audience (qualitative)

In comments, shares, or search queries, we want signals like:

- “기준으로 비교해서 이해하기 쉽다”
- “초보도 용어를 따라갈 수 있다”
- “광고 같지 않다”
- “이 게임이 나에게 맞는지 판단할 수 있었다”

Vanity metrics alone (raw traffic) are not enough in the first 90 days.

---

## Anti-audience (do not optimize for)

- People seeking guaranteed-win schemes
- Readers looking for illegal-access instructions
- Pure bonus hunters with no interest in game quality
- Non-Korean markets as the primary SEO target in v1
