## Top nav (Phase 1)

`슬롯 리뷰` · `슬롯 가이드` · `슬롯 발견`

Utility / static pages sit outside category nav (e.g. 소개, **리뷰 기준/방법론**, disclaimer).

---

## Why these three

### Earns a top-nav seat
- **`reviews`** — Locked Phase 1 main format; carries the mission.
- **`guides`** — Allowed when they directly help understanding/discovery; kept smaller so they don’t steal launch focus.
- **`discover`** — Multi-game curations and comparisons that help readers find games worth checking out; kept at launch by owner decision.

### Cut from launch nav
| Idea | Decision |
|------|----------|
| **랩 노트** | **Removed.** Do not regularly publish articles about our own review process. Handle methodology as a **static page** if needed. |
| Theme / Design / Sound as nav categories | **Reject.** Review lenses / **tags**, not nav buckets. |
| Bonus features / Bonus buy as its own category | **Reject.** Cover inside `reviews`; explain in `guides`; optionally collect in `discover`. |
| Comparisons vs Curations as two categories | **Merged into `discover`.** |
| Misc / General / Other | **Rejected.** |

### Tag vs page vs Phase 2

| Idea | Type | Launch treatment |
|------|------|------------------|
| Theme, provider, bonus-buy, animation-heavy, etc. | **Tag** | Use on posts; not top nav |
| About, **review methodology**, disclaimer, contact | **Page** | Static pages — not categories |
| Provider hubs, dedicated theme navigation, deeper legality/access, operator CTAs | **Phase 2 parking lot** | Outside launch categories |
