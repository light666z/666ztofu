## Why these four (and what we cut)

### Earns a top-nav seat
- **`reviews`** — Locked Phase 1 main format; carries the mission.
- **`guides`** — Allowed when they directly help understanding/discovery; kept smaller so they don’t steal launch focus.
- **`discover`** — Supports the on-site CTA (“explore related reviews”) with experience-angled multi-game paths without inventing a “Misc” bucket.
- **`lab-notes`** — Makes the experience-first method visible; thin volume (5%) so it doesn’t fake a content vertical.

### Overlaps challenged / merged
| Tempting split | Decision |
|----------------|----------|
| Theme / Design / Sound as separate nav categories | **Reject for launch.** Those are review *lenses* and **tags**, not nav buckets—otherwise every review could sit in three categories. |
| Bonus features / Bonus buy as its own category | **Reject for launch.** Cover inside `reviews`; explain the concept in `guides`; optionally collect examples in `discover`. |
| Comparisons vs Curations as two categories | **Merged into `discover`.** Both are multi-game discovery; splitting would create two thin nav items at ~5% each. |
| Providers as a launch category | **Phase 2 parking lot.** Useful later as hubs/tags; not required by locked positioning for Phase 1 nav. |
| 안전/책임 or 이용 가이드 as a category | **Not launch nav.** High-level care stays in static pages/footer if needed—not a monthly content pillar. |
| Misc / General / Other | **Rejected.** |

### Flagged: tag vs page vs Phase 2

| Idea | Type | Launch treatment |
|------|------|------------------|
| Theme, provider, bonus-buy, animation-heavy, etc. | **Tag** | Use on posts; do not put in top nav |
| About, methodology summary, disclaimer, contact | **Page** | Site pages—not categories |
| Provider hubs, deep compare taxonomy, “where to play” later | **Phase 2** | Parked; revisit after review library exists |
| `lab-notes` as nav category | **Confirm** | Could demote to pages only if you prefer a 3-item nav (`reviews` / `guides` / `discover`) |
