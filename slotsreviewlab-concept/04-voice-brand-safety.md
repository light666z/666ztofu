## Tone dials (kept)

Scale: **1 = low / minimal** → **5 = high / strong** (Phase 1 default in bold)

| Dial | Score | Short descriptor |
|------|------:|------------------|
| Formality | **3** | Natural Korean prose; clear and composed, not stiff or slang-heavy |
| Humor | **2–3** | Light playfulness allowed; never sarcastic cruelty or meme-spam |
| Urgency / hype | **1** | No FOMO, no “지금 당장,” no jackpot excitement as persuasion |
| Technical depth | **3** | Enough mechanics detail for curious readers; explain terms when needed; not a math paper |
| Authority posture | **2** | Informed guide, not judge or guru; no absolute verdicts |
| Warmth / address | **3** | Respectful, adult-to-adult; helpful without talking down |

**How we address the reader**
- Default: neutral informative Korean (하다/입니다 mix kept natural and consistent within a post)
- Prefer “독자 / 플레이를 고민하는 분” framing over lecture (“반드시 해야 한다”)
- Dual-access: beginners get plain explanations; experienced readers still get concrete feature/feel detail
- Do **not** invent a catchy second-person nickname for the audience unless you request one

**Language habit (kept)**
- Primary: natural Korean
- English OK for technical terms, brand names, common terminology (explain on first use when helpful)
- Game titles: include commonly used Korean name alongside English when one exists; order by which form is more recognizable

**Recommendation approach (kept):** experience-first (design, theme, visuals, sound, feel, mechanics, bonus features) — not win potential.

## Hard DO / DON’T (brand safety) — locked

### DO

- Recommend based on design, theme, visuals, sound, animations/feel, mechanics, bonus features (incl. bonus buy when present), and overall experience
- Keep a recommendation-first structure while staying non-absolute (“~에게 맞을 수 있다,” “탐험해볼 만하다”)
- Explain unfamiliar terms; stay beginner-accessible without dumbing down
- Use secondary specs (RTP, max win, volatility, paylines, etc.) as factual reference only
- Separate display ads for existing sites from editorial opinion and accuracy; existing sites may be advertised via banners/GIFs, but articles must not be written to promote or recommend those sites
- End with on-site next reads (other SlotReviewLab articles)
- Fact-check game features against notes / available info / official sources when needed
- Allow ordinary factual mentions of slot games, providers, brands, and relevant products when editorially relevant

### DON’T

- Guaranteed-win claims, “필승,” “확실한 수익,” or any implication that a slot reliably makes money
- Misleading probability / payout persuasion (“이 게임은 잘 터진다” as a reason to choose it)
- Let RTP / max win / volatility **determine** the recommendation
- Aggressive gambling-promotion tone or hype CTAs
- Absolute judgments or guarantees in reviews
- In-article CTAs that promote or recommend our advertised existing sites
- Write articles *around* or affiliated with advertised sites
- Create low-value or unrelated content solely to capture search traffic
- Invent screenshots, RTP, features, or play experiences you did not provide
- Publish private individuals’ real names, contact details, or identifiable personal data unless there is a legitimate reason and appropriate permission

### Privacy / people (locked)

- No private individuals’ real names, contact details, or identifiable personal data unless legitimate reason + appropriate permission
- Do not drag unrelated third parties into reviews for color
- Provider / studio / game names are fine (public product names)
- User anecdotes: only if you supply them; strip identifiers unless permission supports naming

### Affiliate / promo rules — Phase 1 clean (locked)

**Phase 1:**
- **Display ads** (banners/GIFs) for *your existing sites* may appear (e.g. sidebar), separate from editorial
- The existence of an advertisement must **not** influence editorial content, accuracy, or opinions
- **No** promotional CTAs or recommendations for those advertised existing sites in the **article body**
- **Allowed:** sitewide disclaimer/footer mentions of the advertising relationship or existing sites
- **Allowed:** ordinary factual editorial mentions of games, providers, brands, and relevant products

**Parked in Phase 2 (ask before enabling) — commercial placement, not normal mentions:**
- Third-party casino/operator **affiliate** links inside articles
- Sponsored posts / paid rankings / paid product placement
- Productized “where to play” commercial modules
- Exact scope of **legality / access** content (**undecided** — owner has not defined Phase 1 scope yet; do not invent one)

## Preferred replacements (don’t → do)

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| 필승 / 무조건 돈 된다 / 수익 보장 | 결과를 보장하지 않음; 경험·연출 기준으로만 추천 |
| 고배당이라 추천 / 맥스윈 때문에 해야 함 | 스펙은 참고로만; 추천 이유는 테마·연출·피처·감각 |
| 지금 바로 플레이하세요 / 놓치면 손해 | 이 게임이 취향에 맞는지 살펴보세요 / 관련 리뷰도 보세요 |
| 이 게임은 잘 터진다 | 보너스 구성·연출 템포가 어떻게 느껴지는지 설명 |
| 공식 1위 / 최고의 슬롯 (근거 없는 절대표현) | ~한 경험을 찾는 사람에게 흥미로울 수 있다 |
| 우리 제휴/광고 사이트에서 하세요 (본문 CTA) | (본문) 다른 SlotReviewLab 글로 이동 · 광고는 편집과 분리 |
| 반드시 이렇게 플레이하라 (권위적 명령) | 초보라면 이 포인트를 먼저 보면 이해하기 쉽다 |
| 인생역전 / 대박 확정 톤 | 차분한 탐험·취향 언어 |
| 미확인 수치를 단정 | 확인된 스펙만; 없으면 “확인된 공식 정보 기준으로는…” / 보류 |
