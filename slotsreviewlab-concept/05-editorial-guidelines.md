# 05 — Editorial Guidelines

## Language policy

- **Primary content language:** Korean
- **English allowed for:** brand names, game titles, provider names, established technical terms (RTP, volatility, Megaways, Buy Bonus, etc.)
- When using an English term, give a short Korean explanation on first use in each article
- Prefer natural Korean over awkward calques

### Example
> 변동성(volatility)은 짧은 구간에서 결과 편차가 얼마나 큰지를 나타내는 개념입니다.

---

## Tone & style

| Attribute | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| Clarity | Short paragraphs; mobile-first scanning |
| Authority | Specific and calm, never arrogant |
| Honesty | Mark unknowns; don’t invent RTP/stats |
| Empathy | Respect beginners; don’t shame lack of knowledge |
| Restraint | No “인생역전”, “무조건 수익”, “오늘만” language |

### Sentence-level preferences
- Prefer concrete verbs over vague hype
- Use tables for comparisons and scores
- Use bullet lists for pros/cons and checklists
- Keep H2/H3 descriptive in Korean

---

## Quality bar (every post must pass)

A draft is publishable only if:

1. **One clear reader job** is solved
2. **Original structure/analysis** exists (not a rewrite of one source)
3. **Facts vs opinions** are distinguishable
4. **Internal links** to methodology/related content are present
5. **No guaranteed-win or illegal-access guidance**
6. **Korean readability** is natural (not machine-translation residue)
7. **Title + intro** match the actual conclusion

---

## Review writing standard

### Required sections
- 한 줄 요약 (quick verdict)
- 점수표 (5 dimensions + total)
- 이런 사람에게 맞음 / 안 맞음
- 핵심 메커니즘
- 보너스·피처 설명
- RTP/변동성 노트 (정보 신뢰도 표시)
- 장점 / 단점
- 최종 평가
- FAQ (3–5)

### Evidence confidence labels
Use one of:
- `공식 정보 기반`
- `널리 통용되는 시장 정보 (교차확인)`
- `편집부 관찰/의견`
- `정보 부족으로 판단 보류`

---

## Sourcing rules

- Prefer primary/official materials when available (provider pages, official sheets)
- Cross-check contested stats with at least two independent references when possible
- Never fabricate paytables, RTP, or release details
- If uncertain, say so and explain what would change the conclusion

---

## Title & metadata conventions

### Title patterns
- Review: `{게임명} 리뷰: {핵심 판단}`
- Guide: `{개념}이란? {독자 가치}`
- Compare: `{A} vs {B}: {결정 기준}`

### Meta description
- 70–110 Korean characters when possible
- Include audience benefit + content type
- Avoid clickbait and money promises

### Featured snippet / intro rule
First 80–120 words should answer the core question directly.

---

## Inclusive & responsible language

**Avoid**
- 중독 미화
- 손실을 “수업료”로 포장하는 표현
- 특정 성별/연령 비하
- 미성년 암시 표현

**Prefer**
- 오락으로서의 선택과 한계를 명확히 쓰기
- 위험 신호를 구체적으로 설명하기
- 독자가 멈추거나 건너뛸 수 있는 선택권을 존중하는 문장

---

## Correction policy (summary)

- Factual errors: correct ASAP, note update timestamp
- Score changes: explain what new information changed the score
- Reader challenges: evaluate on evidence, not ego

## AI-assisted drafting rules (if used)

- Human editor is accountable for final accuracy and tone
- Must rewrite to natural Korean editorial voice
- Must independently verify all numbers and claims
- Must not publish unedited model output
