# 06 — 90-Day Content Plan

## Goal

By day 90:

- Brand identity and niche are publicly consistent
- Categories + IA are live and stable
- **15 quality Korean posts** are published
- Methodology, disclaimer, and responsible pages exist as trust anchors

---

## Cadence

| Phase | Days | Publishing pace | Focus |
|-------|------|-----------------|-------|
| Foundation | 1–14 | 4 posts + core pages | Identity, methodology, beginner hubs |
| Momentum | 15–60 | ~1 post / week (7 posts) | Reviews + supporting clusters |
| Authority | 61–90 | ~1 post / week (4 posts) | Comparisons, provider, trust depth |

Total article target: **15 posts**  
Core static pages (about/methodology/disclaimer/responsible landing) are required infrastructure and counted separately from the 15.

Suggested rhythm: **draft → edit → fact-check → publish** with at least 1 buffer day before each publish date.

---

## Core pages to ship before/at launch

1. `/methodology/` — 리뷰 기준과 점수 체계
2. `/about/` — 소개
3. `/responsible/` — 안전/책임 랜딩
4. `/disclaimer/` — 정보성 콘텐츠 고지
5. Homepage start paths wired to first posts

## 90-day success review questions

1. Can a stranger explain our niche in one sentence after visiting home?
2. Are the 5 main categories still clean (no taxonomy sprawl)?
3. Do 15 posts follow the same quality bar?
4. Which 3 posts earned genuine save/share/return behavior?
5. What topic gaps should quarter 2 fill — without abandoning the niche?
