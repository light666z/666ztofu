# 07 — SEO & Keyword Strategy (Korean Market)

## SEO philosophy for v1

Prioritize **genuine value for Korean readers** over raw traffic chasing.

That means:
- Cover questions people actually ask in Korean
- Build topical authority with hub-and-spoke clusters
- Win trust snippets with clear definitions and tables
- Avoid doorway pages and near-duplicate “카지노 추천” spam patterns

## On-page standards

1. **One primary intent per URL**
2. H1 matches reader promise; do not bait-and-switch
3. Use descriptive H2s in natural Korean
4. Place definition answer near the top for explainer posts
5. Include FAQ block for long-tail questions
6. Image alt text in Korean, functional not keyword-stuffed
7. Canonical every indexable post

### Title tag pattern
`{핵심 키워드} | slotreviewlab`

Examples:
- `슬롯 RTP란? 환수율 바로 읽기 | slotreviewlab`
- `Sweet Bonanza 리뷰: 초보 친화성 점수 | slotreviewlab`

## Internal linking for topical authority

```text
Pillar hub
  ├─ concept spoke A
  ├─ concept spoke B
  └─ example review spokes
Review spoke → methodology + provider + related concept
Responsible spoke → homepage + methodology + relevant guides
```

Minimum links:
- New post → 2 contextual internal links
- Updated hub → link outward to each new spoke

- [ ] Fast LCP on home/review templates

- [ ] `robots.txt` allows key sections
- [ ] Open Graph titles/descriptions in Korean
- [ ] Structured data: `Article` / `FAQPage` where appropriate
- [ ] Noindex thin tag archives if they duplicate category pages

## Content vs search intent alignment

| Intent | Do | Don’t |
|--------|----|-------|
| Informational | Teach clearly | Force affiliate CTA early |
| Commercial investigation | Compare with criteria | Fake “공식 1위” claims |
| Navigational | Make brand pages obvious | Stuff unrelated keywords |
| Harm-avoidance | Give checklists | Scare-clickbait |

## Measurement for first 90 days

Track lightly; optimize after content exists.

**Leading indicators**
- Indexed pages count
- Average engagement time on guides/reviews
- Internal link click-through from hubs

**Lagging indicators**
- Queries brand + non-brand impressions
- Returning visitor rate
- Scroll depth on review template

Do **not** judge success only by total sessions in month 1–2.
