# 08 — Trust, Compliance Framing & Monetization Boundaries

> This document is an **editorial framing guide**, not legal advice. Obtain qualified legal counsel before monetization, advertising claims, or jurisdiction-specific statements.

---

## Trust position

slotreviewlab earns the right to recommend *how to evaluate games* only if readers believe we will not manipulate them.

Trust stack for v1:
1. Transparent methodology
2. Consistent scoring
3. Clear separation of facts vs opinions
4. Visible responsible-play stance
5. Honest disclosure of commercial relationships

---

## Non-negotiable content boundaries

### Never publish
- Instructions for bypassing Korean gambling laws or access restrictions
- Guaranteed profit / “필승 전략” claims
- Content targeting minors or using youth-oriented framing
- Fake urgency around deposits or bonuses
- Unverified “공식 환수율/당첨 인증” screenshots used as proof of winnings
- Impersonation of regulators or licensed operators

### Always include where relevant
- Informational/entertainment purpose language
- Uncertainty labels for incomplete data
- Responsible-play reminders on review templates and trust pages
- Disclosure when a post has commercial relationships

---

## Korean-market sensitivity (editorial stance)

Because the audience is in South Korea, editorial defaults are:

1. **Explain games and concepts** rather than pushing play actions
2. Prefer “이해/비교/판단” CTAs over “지금 플레이”
3. Cover scam literacy and overpromise detection early in the content library
4. Avoid presenting offshore gambling access as a normal consumer tip
5. Keep tone adult, sober, and non-glamorizing

If a draft’s main value depends on facilitating illegal participation, **kill the draft**.

### No-affiliate pure editorial
> 이 글은 제휴 관계 없이 작성된 편집 콘텐츠입니다.

Place disclosures **near the top**, not only in the footer.

## Monetization boundaries (v1)

Allowed to explore later (after trust pages + 10 posts exist):
- Display ads with category controls
- Sponsored explainers clearly labeled as sponsored
- Affiliate relationships **only** where legally appropriate and clearly disclosed
- Digital products that teach evaluation literacy (checklists, templates)

Not allowed in v1 brand building:
- Selling “winning systems”
- Paid rankings that override methodology
- Dark-pattern email sequences pushing deposits
- Unlabeled native advertising

### Monetization readiness gate
Do not scale monetization until:
- [ ] Methodology public
- [ ] 10+ posts meet quality bar
- [ ] Disclosure template standardized
- [ ] Legal review of offer types completed

---

## Review integrity rules

1. Money never changes dimension weights
2. Sponsored posts cannot be silently mixed into organic reviews
3. If a commercial partner’s game is reviewed, apply the same template and show the relationship
4. Scores may be updated; score history should be noted when material

## Correction & complaint handling

| Case | Action |
|------|--------|
| Factual error | Correct + timestamp + brief note |
| Reader disputes a score | Re-check evidence; explain keep/change |
| Disclosure missing | Add disclosure and review process |
| Legal takedown / serious claim | Unpublished pending review; escalate to human owner |
