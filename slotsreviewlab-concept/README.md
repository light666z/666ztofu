## Purpose

This folder is the foundational concept pack for **slotreviewlab**: a Korean-market blog that helps readers understand slot games with clear reviews, practical explanations, and responsible guidance — not hype-driven casino promotion.

Success in 90 days means:

1. A clear blog identity and niche are locked
2. Main categories and site structure are finalized
3. 10–15 quality Korean posts are published consistently

## One-line positioning

**slotreviewlab** is a Korean-language “review lab” that explains slot games with transparent criteria, so readers can make informed entertainment decisions without being pressured by affiliate hype.
