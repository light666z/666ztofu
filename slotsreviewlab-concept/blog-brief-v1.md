## 3. Positioning

### Mission
SlotReviewLab helps Korean adults decide whether a featured slot is interesting or worth checking out based on its design, features, mechanics, theme, sound, and overall experience — not its potential winnings.

**Reader outcome:** A discovery judgment (“interesting / worth checking out or not”) driven by experience characteristics, not money outcomes.

### Audience
- **Who:** Korean adults interested in slot games who want interesting titles worth checking out (visual design, features, themes, overall experience).
- **Expertise:** Dual-access — beginners can follow; experienced readers still get useful detail. Unfamiliar terms are explained when needed.
- **Not primary for:** Foreign-language audiences as the main target (others may still read).
- **Not for:** Readers seeking guaranteed wins, “sure profit,” or aggressive gambling promotion over informative exploration.

### Differentiator (priority, not uniqueness claim)
Reviews are **experience-first**. Recommendations answer whether a game is visually appealing, interesting, unique, or enjoyable to explore (design, theme, sound, animations/feel, mechanics, bonus features including bonus buy when present). Specs such as RTP, max win, volatility, and paylines may appear as **secondary factual reference** and must not determine the recommendation.

### Commercial boundary (Phase 1)
Display ads (banners/GIFs) for the operator’s existing sites may appear (e.g. sidebar). Ads must not influence editorial content. Article bodies must not contain promotional CTAs or recommendations for those advertised sites. Sitewide disclaimer/footer mentions of the advertising relationship are allowed. On-site editorial CTAs point to other SlotReviewLab articles only.

### Game selection (Phase 1)
Games may be chosen from internal notes, play experience, interesting or new releases, and games with unique features or designs worth covering — not limited to one pipeline.

### Out of scope / constraints (Phase 1 launch)
- Low-value or unrelated content created solely to capture search traffic
- Recommendations framed around expected winnings, RTP, or max-win potential
- Guaranteed-win language, misleading probability/payout persuasion, profit reliability claims
- Absolute judgments or guarantee-style tone
- Article content written around or affiliated with advertised sites; in-body CTAs to those sites
- Shop, contact page, newsletter/membership portals, provider/theme nav hubs
- In-article commercial affiliate / “where to play” operator modules
- Treat **legality/access content scope** as undecided (see Open questions / Phase 2) — do not invent a Phase 1 legality content program

## 5. Voice + brand safety

### Tone dials (1–5)
| Dial | Score | Notes |
|------|------:|-------|
| Formality | 3 | Clear, composed natural Korean |
| Humor | 2–3 | Light playfulness; no cruel sarcasm |
| Urgency / hype | 1 | No FOMO |
| Technical depth | 3 | Enough detail; not a math paper |
| Authority posture | 2 | Informed guide, not guru |
| Warmth / address | 3 | Adult-to-adult; not lecturing |

### DO
- Recommend on design, theme, visuals, sound, feel, mechanics, bonus features (incl. bonus buy when present)
- Recommendation-first, non-absolute language
- Explain unfamiliar terms; dual-access readability
- Specs as secondary facts only
- Keep display ads separate from editorial; no in-body promo CTAs for advertised sites
- End with on-site next reads
- Fact-check features from notes / available info / official sources when needed
- Allow ordinary factual mentions of games, providers, brands, and relevant products

### DON’T
- Guaranteed wins, profit promises, “필승,” or reliable-money implications
- Misleading probability/payout persuasion as a reason to choose a game
- Let RTP / max win / volatility determine the recommendation
- Aggressive gambling hype CTAs; absolute judgments
- In-article CTAs for advertised existing sites; write articles around those sites
- Create low-value or unrelated content solely for search traffic
- Invent facts, specs, screenshots, or play experiences not provided
- Publish private individuals’ real names, contact details, or identifiable personal data without legitimate reason and appropriate permission

### Phrasing replacements (summary)
Avoid win/hype/absolute/operator-CTA language; prefer experience, taste, and non-guaranteed discovery language (full pairs in `04-voice-brand-safety.md`).

## 6. Content system

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Baseline | **3 posts / week** (KST) |
| Monthly target | **~12 posts** |
| Stretch | Faster (including optional daily) only with approved draft buffer (prefer 2–4 queued) |

| Workflow | AI draft → human edit → owner final approval → publish |

### Formats → categories
| Format | Category |
|--------|----------|
| Experience review | `reviews` |
| How-to explainer | `guides` |
| Observation curation | `discover` |

### Length bands (Korean characters, spaces included)
| Format | Target | Ceiling |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Experience review | 2,800–4,200자 | ~5,000자 |
| How-to explainer | 2,200–3,500자 | ~4,200자 |

1. Hook + reader job

4. Secondary specs when useful  
5. Who it suits / doesn’t  
6. Calm close (non-absolute)  
7. On-site next reads only  
8. Optional short FAQ if clarifying

- **Primary SEO** — main organic target when evidence supports  
- **Secondary / long-tail** — supporting phrases  
- **Editorial topic** — publish for value; not a major traffic driver  

No numerical search volumes are assigned.

### Primary SEO keywords (locked)
| Category | Primary SEO |
|----------|-------------|
| 슬롯 리뷰 | `{게임명} 리뷰` · `{게임명} 슬롯 리뷰` |
| 슬롯 가이드 | `슬롯 보너스 구매` · `슬롯 고르는 법` |
| 슬롯 발견 | `{게임 A} vs {게임 B}` (demand depends on the specific games’ popularity) · `슬롯 비교` |

### Not primary (kept in strategy)
| Item | Class |
|------|--------|
| `{한국어명} 리뷰` when KR name is commonly used | Variant |
| `보너스 구매란` | Secondary / long-tail |
| `흥미로운 슬롯 찾는 법` | Editorial topic |
| `연출 좋은 슬롯` | Editorial topic |

### Rejected as primary SEO targets
Payout-led hooks (`고배당…`, `맥스윈…`), win hype (`필승`, `잘 터지는…`), operator funnels (`슬롯사이트 추천`, `여기서 플레이`), bare `슬롯 추천`, unrelated SEO bait.

### Title / meta (summary)
- Title formulas: experience-question reviews; concept explainers; taste guides; A vs B feel comparisons; curated experience lists — no hype/guarantee/operator CTAs  
- Meta: ~70–110 Korean characters; experience-led; no stuffing or win promises  

### Channel lean
Reviews mainly search-led (game names); guides mixed; discover mainly internal/social/browse.
